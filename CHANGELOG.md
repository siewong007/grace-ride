# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
- Fixed fares between service areas. The **Head of Traffic** (and admins) can now set them, under **Settings → Fares**: pick an area, then fill in its fare to each other open area (and within the area), the same in both directions. Unpriced routes show **Not set**, with a count per area and an overall "x of y routes priced". New permission `admin_fares` (view, update): Head of Traffic and admin can edit; President and Vice-President can view; other roles get it only if an admin grants it on the Roles page. Fares are no longer gated by `admin_settings`. Migration `0022_default_service_areas.sql` adds the frequent driver areas UT, Sec 17, UM, KK and Pacific/RM (no fares set; existing areas untouched). Drivers and staff now see a ride's **Fixed fare** (pickup area ↔ event area) on the ride page and in the driver's ride list, or "Not set yet" for a claimable event whose route has no fare.
- On phones, swiping left or right now moves only between the bottom tabs (Home, Events and Profile, plus Admin for staff) and does not wrap around. On a sub-page, the swipe first follows in-app history in that direction, and only then switches to the neighbouring tab.
- Driver mileage reimbursement is now a flat fare per pair of areas (e.g. Cheras ↔ PJ, the same both ways) instead of distance × a per-km rate. Admins set the fares under **Settings → Rates & limits**; organisers pick an **Event area** on the event form, and a completed ride on a mileage-claimable event pays the fare for (pickup area ↔ event area). One driver trip pays its highest fare once, however many riders it carried. A route with no fare yet pays nothing: the driver sees a prompt on the ride to hand in a mileage receipt (`POST /reimbursements` with `kind: "mileage"`), and when review approves it the ride carries the confirmed amount. The per-km rates (`reimbursement_rates`, `/admin/settings/rates`) are removed; migration `0016_area_fares.sql`.
- Rides and driving now start from an event. **My rides** and **Driving** are no longer in the menu or tab bar; a member signs up for an event first, then asks for a ride (or, for drivers, offers to drive) on that event's page, where a driver sees only that event's requests. The server enforces it: `POST /rides` needs an `event_id` the member is going to, and a driver may claim a ride only for an event they are going to (`EVENT_RIDE_NEEDS_SIGNUP`, `EVENT_DRIVE_NEEDS_SIGNUP`). Ride history and driving history (schedule, vehicle and areas) live under **Profile → Related pages**. The non-event recurring ride and standing driver schedule screens are removed; recurring events cover that need.
- Profile page refresh: a larger avatar card at the top, and each section row shows a one-line summary (phone, push and reminder settings, passkeys and 2FA). Tap the avatar to change it: upload or take a photo and crop it to a circle (it's resized in the browser, which also drops its location data), or show your initial or an icon on a colour you choose. Photos can now be removed. The chosen avatar also appears in the top bar, on the lock screen, on the ride driver card, and in the admin member list.
- Trip chat: members can react to a message with an emoji (👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏; one per person, tap again to remove), and their own messages show WhatsApp-style ticks: one tick sent, two ticks delivered to every other trip member's app, two coloured ticks once everyone has read it. Staff viewing a chat don't count towards delivered or read.
- Announcements can be sent to chosen member groups (and their sub-groups). Only those members see them on Home and get the notification; leaving every group unticked still reaches everyone. Admins can change an announcement's groups when editing it.
